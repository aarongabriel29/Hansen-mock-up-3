Details & Specifications

Standard pricing covers depths from 3.5 feet in the shallow cove to 5.5 or 6 feet in the main basin. Custom sports pools, deep ends up to 8 feet with diving requirements, or infinity perimeter overflows are calculated following soil checks.
Standard package pricing applies to flat to moderately sloping rear yards with at least 8 feet of machine access clearance from the street. Properties requiring caissons, major hillside retaining walls, or hand-dig operations receive transparent line-item assessments upfront.
We partner directly with Lyon Financial and HFS Financial. Unsecured swimming pool loans up to $150,000 are available with terms extending to 15 or 20 years, without home equity requirements or prepayment penalties.
Every Hansen waterscape includes a Lifetime Structural Shotcrete Shell Warranty, a 3-year bumper-to-bumper equipment warranty through our certified installer status, and a 10-year pebble/quartz interior finish warranty.

Questions homeowners ask us

Everything you need to know about pricing, timeline, and Ventura County site conditions.

The primary variables are yard access (under 8ft requires smaller specialized mini-excavators or bobcats), sloped hillsides requiring structural friction piles or engineering retaining walls, excessive utility line relocations, or homeowner additions such as expansive interlocking pavers or solid patio covers.
From the moment excavation breaks ground, a standard Hansen pool and spa build runs 8 to 12 weeks. City permitting typically adds 4 to 8 weeks beforehand depending on whether your jurisdiction is City of Moorpark, Thousand Oaks, or Ventura County Building & Safety.
Hansen Waterscapes manages 100% of the architectural plan creation, structural engineering calculations, city filings, revisions, and on-site city inspector walkthroughs. You never have to stand in line at city hall.
We engineer all shells with 4,000 PSI high-density pneumatically applied shotcrete (never low-grade pool plaster or cheap gunite blends) coupled with dense #4 steel rebar cages on tight 8-inch grids. This prevents structural shifting during wet winter and dry summer expansion cycles.
